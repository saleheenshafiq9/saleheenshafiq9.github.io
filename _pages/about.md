I'm a PhD student in Computer Science at **Purdue University**, where I joined the [PurSec Lab](https://pursec.cs.purdue.edu/) in Fall 2025. My research examines the security of **autonomous and cyber-physical systems**, particularly unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs).

I'm fascinated by the security challenges that arise when software interacts with the physical world. As UAVs become more common in defense, industry, and agriculture, software defects can have direct physical consequences. I develop techniques for analyzing and testing drone software through **fuzzing, reverse engineering, binary analysis, and patch analysis** to find vulnerabilities.

Previously, I was a Software Engineer at **Samsung R&D Institute Bangladesh**. I hold a B.Sc. in Software Engineering from the **University of Dhaka**, graduating with a CGPA of **3.86/4.00**.

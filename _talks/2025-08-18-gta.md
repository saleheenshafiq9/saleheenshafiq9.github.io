I am starting my PhD journey as of today. I’m joining Purdue University’s PurSec Lab from the Fall 2025.

My research will focus on software security and privacy, integrating human factors to develop systems that are both reliable and accessible. I’m also curious about the evolving landscape of Generative AI, the challenges and risks tied to its adoption with systems.

I am also going to be a GTA for Fall 2025 semester. I will be teaching CS251 (Data Structures & Algorithms).
